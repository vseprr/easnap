# Easnap v1.1.0-rc.1

A release candidate, prepared on 2026-11-07 on the branch `stabilize/v1.1.0`
and checked by the owner on the development PC. It was **not published**.
The owner's check found two problems, both corrected before release: a second
submenu arrow in the tray menu, and the caption Windows painted over Settings
and the message dialogs when they were moved. v1.1.0 was then prepared from
the corrected sources, without a second candidate.

What the candidate contained is in the [notes of v1.1.0](v1.1.0.md); its runs
are in the [validation records](validation-history.md#validation-of-v110-rc1).
