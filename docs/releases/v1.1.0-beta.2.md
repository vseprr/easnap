# Easnap v1.1.0-beta.2

The second beta of v1.1. It fixes what testing of the first beta found: black
boxes that Easnap's own windows left in screenshots and recordings, a tools
island that jumped away from the frame, a notice cut off in Turkish, and
recordings that the trim window refused on computers without a hardware video
encoder.

This is a prerelease. The latest stable version is v1.0.2. A beta installs
over a stable version or an earlier beta and uses the same settings; to go
back, install v1.0.2 again.

## Fixed

- **Black boxes in pictures.** Easnap's islands (the screenshot tools, the
  recording panel, the Scrollshot island and the "Copied"/"Saved" notices)
  are never part of a picture. Windows could nevertheless leave a black box of
  their shape where one had just been shown again or moved, as long as nothing
  under it changed. A screenshot taken after moving the frame under the tools
  island held such a box, and a recording could show the recording panel as a
  black box. This no longer happens.
- **Tooltips in recordings.** The tooltip of a button on the recording panel
  could appear in a recording when the panel lay over the recorded area.
- **The tools island jumped.** Choosing a tool on a frame near the bottom of
  the screen sent the tools to the other side of the frame. They now keep
  their place: room for the tool's options is kept from the start.
- **"Saved to your screenshots folder" was cut off** in Turkish. The Copied
  and Saved notices now fit their text in every language.
- **Recordings on computers without a hardware H.264 encoder** were stamped
  one frame late, and the trim window could refuse to preview them. Easnap now
  asks the software encoder to keep the frames in order.

## Changed

- During a Scrollshot, a **right-click** outside the frame works like Esc: it
  discards, and asks first when you have scrolled for an image. Before, it did
  nothing.

Both packages contain the same two executables. Easnap needs Windows 10
version 2004 or later, x64. The binaries and the installer are **unsigned**,
so Windows may display a publisher or reputation warning.

To check a download:

```powershell
Get-FileHash .\Easnap-v1.1.0-beta.2-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256
gh attestation verify .\Easnap-v1.1.0-beta.2-windows-x64-setup.exe --repo vseprr/easnap
```

Exit Easnap before installing over, or replacing, an existing copy. The
installer's language applies to its own pages; Easnap follows the language of
Windows until you choose one in Settings.

## Validation

- **Test lab** (a Windows 10 22H2 virtual machine, one 1920x1080 display at
  125 %, no GPU): the Full tier passed 48 of 48 on the sources of this version,
  before the version number was raised. A new test,
  `island_capture_exclusion`, shows each island and a tooltip over a still
  desktop, shows them again after hiding them and moves them, and reads the
  screen the way a screenshot and a recording read it; before the fix the
  tools island left 46,744 black pixels, after it none.
- **Development PC** (Windows 10 22H2, two 1920x1080 displays at 125 %, NVIDIA
  GPU): the black box after moving the frame under the tools island was
  reproduced by hand with the first beta and is gone with this version.
- The Release workflow builds and tests the published binaries on a hosted
  runner.

Not tested: Windows 11, a Windows N edition, scale factors other than 125 %,
Scrollshot on real applications, and the package-manager manifests. The
installer and its uninstall questions are tested by hand after publication.

## Known issues

- Scrollshot follows content that moves only when you scroll. Video,
  animations or a list that loads more while it scrolls inside the frame can
  make it lose track; narrow the frame.
- Copying and saving a very tall image take a moment during which the island
  does not respond.
- A screenshot can differ from the screen by one level at a few colour values
  while the workspace is visible. Scrollshot is not affected.
- Transient redraw artifacts can occur while resizing or maximizing the Trim
  window.

See the [notes of the first beta](v1.1.0-beta.1.md) for everything v1.1 adds,
and [troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md) for the questions that come up
most often.
